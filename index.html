Questions

Straight answers

Where is my resume stored?

In your browser, on your device. ATS Resume has no accounts and no database. Use Builder, then More, to export a backup file, import one, or delete the saved copy.

Will this get me past an applicant tracking system?

It helps you avoid common problems: complex layouts, images of text and unusual symbols. No tool can promise how a specific employer's system will rank you, and the readiness score is based on common parsing and writing rules, not a particular vendor.

Does it use AI?

No. The writing hints and keyword matching are simple rules that run in your browser. They point out things like passive openers and missing numbers, and they never invent facts about your experience.

Why is the PDF text selectable?

The PDF is created with text and drawing commands rather than a screenshot of the page. That means the words can be searched, copied and read by parsers.

Does it work offline?

Yes, almost entirely. The editor, preview, saving and PDF download all work without a connection. Only the site fonts load from the internet, and they fall back to system fonts when offline.

Ready to build a resume that reads cleanly?

It takes a few minutes, needs no account, and your draft is saved on this device as you type.
