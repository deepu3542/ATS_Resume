How the score works

100 points, no black box

The score follows a best-practice ATS checklist. Real applicant tracking systems do not publish how they rank candidates, so no tool can compute an exact employer score. Treat this as a clear, repeatable guide to what to fix.

CategoryPointsWhat is checked
Keywords40 ptsExact phrases from the posting (17), 8 to 15 keywords matched (5), keywords spread across Summary, Skills and Work Experience (6), exact wording such as "Power BI" not "PowerBI" (3), acronym plus full term (3), no keyword stuffing (3), exact job title (3).
Formatting20 ptsSingle column with no tables, text boxes or graphics (5), standard section headings (6), standard font at 10 to 12 pt (3), contact details in the body (3), safe file type (3).
Content40 ptsName (1), email (3), phone (2), work experience (6), dates (2), education (5), skills (5), action verbs (5), quantified achievements (6), certifications spelled out (3), summary (1), length (1).

Without a job description, keyword checks are skipped and the score covers the other 60 points scaled to 100. Checks that cannot be measured from pasted text (such as fonts) are left out instead of guessed.
