Privacy policy

Your resume stays on your device.

This policy explains what ATS Resume stores, what it does not collect, and which outside services your browser contacts. Last updated October 16, 2026.

Summary

ATS Resume is a website that runs in your browser. You type your resume into it, and it is saved in your own browser storage. We do not have accounts, we do not run a database of resumes, and we do not receive what you type.

In short: no sign-up, no upload of your resume, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking scripts on this site.

What is stored on your device

To keep your draft between visits, the site uses your browser’s storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

ItemWherePurposeHow to remove
Your resume draft (name, contact details, experience, education, skills, projects, job description text, template and paper choice)Local storageLets you continue editing laterBuilder, then More, then Delete saved copy. Or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
A flag noting that the loading animation has playedSession storageAvoids replaying the intro on every pageRemoved automatically when you close the tab.

Files you choose in the ATS checker (.docx or .txt) are read inside your browser and are never uploaded.

The site does not set cookies. Clearing your browser data, using a different browser or device, or private browsing can make your saved draft disappear, so use Export backup in the builder if the draft matters to you.

What we do not collect

  • The content of your resume. It is processed in your browser only, including the preview, the ATS checks, the writing hints and the PDF.
  • Accounts, passwords or payment details. The site has none.
  • Analytics, advertising or tracking data. No such scripts are included.

The company or person who hosts this website may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, under their own policy. We do not use those logs to look at your resume, because your resume is never sent to the server.

Third-party services

One outside service is loaded when you use the site. When your browser requests it, the provider can see technical details such as your IP address and browser type, under its own privacy policy.

ServiceWhy it is usedData involved
Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com)Displays the site’s typefacesYour IP address and browser details are sent with the font request. No resume content.

The PDF is created entirely in your browser by code that ships with the site, so no PDF library is requested from outside. If the site owner hosts the fonts themselves, the font request will no longer go to Google either.

Contacting us

The contact form does not send data to a server. When you submit it, the page prepares an email and asks your email app to open it. Nothing is sent until you press Send there. If you do send it, your name, email address and message are handled by your email provider and by us as the recipient, and we use them only to reply to you.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: use Delete saved copy in the builder, or clear this site’s data in your browser.
  • Keep a copy: use Export backup to save your resume as a file, and Import backup to load it again.
  • Avoid outside requests: a content blocker can stop the font request. Everything will still work, and the site will use your system fonts instead.

Because we do not hold your resume or an account for you, there is no personal data of that kind on our side to access, correct or delete. If you emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, write to us using the address below.

Children

The site is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the way the site works changes, for example if analytics or accounts are added, this policy will be updated and the date at the top will change.
